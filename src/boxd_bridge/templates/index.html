Why there is no live scrobbling

Letterboxd's write API is a request-only closed beta, so no self-hosted tool can push entries to your diary as you watch. Their CSV importer is idempotent: re-importing the same history updates matching diary entries rather than duplicating them. This exports your whole history every time, which is why there is no sync state to manage.
